# TownOS

> **One Tap. A Safer Everyday.**
>
> A smart township safety platform that uses battery-free NFC tags and local edge infrastructure to improve pedestrian safety, school arrival verification, emergency response, and family wellbeing.

## 🚀 Overview

TownOS is designed for modern communities such as **City of Elmina**, providing inclusive smart-city services without requiring smartphones, GPS trackers, or rechargeable wearables.

Residents carry passive NFC stickers on everyday items like:

- 🎒 School bags
- 👓 Glasses
- 🦯 Walking canes
- 🏃 Sports bands

These battery-free tags can interact with township infrastructure and smartphones to enable safer daily living.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚸 Smart Crosswalk Assist
Automatically extends pedestrian crossing time for seniors and mobility-impaired residents.

### 🎒 School Arrival Verification
Notifies parents when children safely arrive at school.

### 🌤 Weather Risk Alerts
Sends heat and weather warnings for vulnerable family members.

### 🚨 Emergency NFC Medical Card
Allows bystanders to access emergency instructions and contact family instantly.

### 🤖 AI Risk Assessor
Converts health conditions into personalized safety recommendations.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support
English, Bahasa Melayu, and Simplified Chinese.
